# Website Design

The storefront now uses a cohesive editorial retail system built around Yale blue, warm gold, crisp white, and a restrained coral accent. Serif display headings preserve the collegiate character while the sans-serif interface text keeps shopping controls easy to scan.

Product cards now lift subtly, reveal a small collection label, and use a gold edge on hover. Catalogue controls sit in a distinct white toolbar, product detail photography has stronger framing, and account forms have clearer visual separation. These changes make products and actions easier to identify and give customers more confidence that the shop is complete and trustworthy.

The chat button and panel now feel like part of the storefront through matching colors, a gold accent, depth, and a short opening transition. Buttons, links, fields, and selects have consistent hover and keyboard-focus feedback. Motion is automatically minimized for shoppers who prefer reduced motion.
